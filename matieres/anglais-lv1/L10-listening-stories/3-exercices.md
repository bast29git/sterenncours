::: plan
- **Exercices 1 à 6** : niveau *Application* : prétérit, connecteurs, frise, première nouvelle
- **Exercices 7 à 11** : niveau *Entraînement* : récits complets, bulletins, interviews courtes
- **Exercices 12 à 15** : niveau *Approfondissement* : inférences, ton, résumé rédigé
- **Exercice 16** : sujet de type devoir, noté sur 20
:::

::: materiel
- Cette fiche ouverte dans le site, pour le bouton **Écouter** de chaque script
- Le cahier d'anglais ou le cahier à imprimer de la leçon
- Un crayon et une règle
:::

::: info Le repère de support, en tête de chaque exercice
**💻 sur écran** : les quatre premiers exercices, faits **ensemble** pendant la séance, corrigés à voix haute juste après.
**✍️ à la main** : tous les autres, faits **seule** : on écoute le script dans le site, on écrit les réponses sur papier. Le script imprimé n'apparaît pas dans le cahier.
:::

::: info La règle des deux écoutes
Première écoute sans rien noter, ou avec un mot par case pour un récit. Deuxième écoute pour les détails. Une troisième écoute est permise pour vérifier. On lit le script **seulement après** avoir répondu.
:::

## Niveau Application : exercices 1 à 6

::: exercice 1 | application | 4 min | ecran
Écouter les six verbes et les classer selon le son de la terminaison : /t/, /d/ ou /ɪd/.

::: audio en Six verbs
Stopped. Played. Wanted. Watched. Called. Needed.
:::

::: corrige
**/t/** : stopped, watched (après p et tch, sons sourds). **/d/** : played, called (après une voyelle et l, sons sonores). **/ɪd/** : wanted, needed (après t et d : une syllabe de plus).
:::
:::

::: exercice 2 | application | 4 min | ecran
Écouter et noter les cinq verbes irréguliers au prétérit, puis donner leur base verbale.

::: audio en Irregular verbs
I woke up late, ran to the station, took the wrong train, and got off in the wrong town. In the end I found a bus home.
:::

::: corrige
**woke up** (wake up), **ran** (run), **took** (take), **got off** (get off), **found** (find).
Aucun -ed à entendre : ce sont les formes irrégulières et le repère « in the end » qui disent le passé.
:::
:::

::: exercice 3 | application | 4 min | ecran
Écouter et noter les connecteurs dans l'ordre. Les classer : début, suite, rupture, fin. Comme dans un manga, l'histoire a un début, une rupture et une fin.

::: audio en Connectors
At first the party was boring. Then my cousin arrived with her guitar. Suddenly everyone was singing. Finally, at midnight, the neighbours asked us to stop.
:::

::: corrige
**At first** (début), **then** (suite), **suddenly** (rupture), **finally** (fin).
Quatre connecteurs, quatre cases de frise : ennui, arrivée de la cousine, tout le monde chante, les voisins font arrêter.
:::
:::

::: exercice 4 | application | 5 min | ecran
Écouter le récit. Tracer une frise de cinq cases et écrire un mot par case. Puis raconter en anglais en trois phrases.

::: audio en The lost dog
Last Sunday our dog Max disappeared from the garden. First we looked in the street, then in the park. After an hour, a neighbour called: Max was in her kitchen, eating her cat's food. In the end we laughed, but Max is not allowed in the garden alone any more.
:::

::: corrige
Frise : 1. **disappeared** (garden, last Sunday). 2. **street**. 3. **park**. 4. **neighbour** (kitchen, cat's food). 5. **laughed** / not alone any more.
Trois phrases : « Last Sunday the dog disappeared from the garden. The family looked in the street and in the park. A neighbour found him in her kitchen eating the cat's food. »
:::
:::

::: exercice 5 | application | 5 min | main
Sterenn suit les nouvelles d'une école anglaise avec laquelle sa classe échange des lettres et des mangas. Écouter la nouvelle et répondre aux cinq questions : who, what, where, when, why.

::: audio en A school news item
Students at Riverside School planted two hundred trees in the park behind the school on Friday morning. The head teacher said the project was a response to last summer's heatwave, to give the town more shade.
:::

::: corrige
Who : **students at Riverside School** (and the head teacher). What : **planted two hundred trees**. Where : **in the park behind the school**. When : **on Friday morning**. Why : **because of last summer's heatwave, to give more shade**.
La cause est annoncée par « a response to » : c'est un signal de « why ».
:::
:::

::: exercice 6 | application | 5 min | main
Écouter les quatre phrases. Pour chacune, dire si c'est un fait, une opinion ou un sentiment, et donner le mot-signal.

::: audio en Fact, opinion or feeling?
One: The concert lasted two hours. Two: In my opinion, the second band was better. Three: I was so happy when they played my favourite song. Four: To be honest, the tickets were too expensive.
:::

::: corrige
1. **Fait** : « lasted two hours » (chiffre, prétérit). 2. **Opinion** : « in my opinion ». 3. **Sentiment** : « I was so happy ». 4. **Opinion** : « to be honest », « too expensive ».
:::
:::

## Niveau Entraînement : exercices 7 à 11

::: exercice 7 | entrainement | 6 min | main
Écouter le récit. Remettre les cinq événements dans l'ordre : the phone rang ; she missed the bus ; she woke up late ; her teacher was ill ; she ran to school.

::: audio en A strange morning
Yesterday I woke up late because my alarm didn't ring. I ran to the bus stop, but the bus had just left, so I missed it. I ran all the way to school. When I arrived, the phone in the office rang: it was my teacher, saying she was ill and there was no lesson. I could have stayed in bed!
:::

::: corrige
1. **She woke up late.** 2. **She missed the bus.** 3. **She ran to school.** 4. **The phone rang.** 5. **Her teacher was ill.**
Le dernier événement (la prof malade) est appris **par** le coup de téléphone : il vient donc après.
:::
:::

::: exercice 8 | entrainement | 6 min | main
Sterenn lit les nouvelles anglaises pendant son voyage vers les aurores boréales. Écouter le bulletin. Compléter le tableau pour les deux nouvelles : what happened, where, numbers.

::: audio en Two news items
Firstly, a fire damaged a bakery in the centre of Bristol early this morning. Nobody was hurt, but the shop will be closed for at least three weeks. And in weather news, temperatures reached thirty-four degrees in London yesterday, the hottest day of the year so far. Doctors advise people to drink water regularly and to stay out of the sun between noon and four.
:::

::: corrige
| | Nouvelle 1 | Nouvelle 2 |
|---|---|---|
| What | **a fire damaged a bakery** | **the hottest day of the year** |
| Where | **centre of Bristol** | **London** |
| Numbers | closed for **three weeks** ; **nobody** hurt | **34 degrees** ; stay out of the sun **between noon and four** |
:::
:::

::: exercice 9 | entrainement | 7 min | main
Écouter l'interview. Relever deux faits, une opinion et un sentiment, avec les mots qui les signalent.

::: audio en Interview with a swimmer
Interviewer: You won your first national medal last month. How did it feel?
Swimmer: Honestly, I couldn't believe it. I've trained six days a week for four years, so when I touched the wall and saw the time, I just cried.
Interviewer: What's next?
Swimmer: The European championships in June. I think I can reach the final, but the others are very strong.
:::

::: corrige
Faits : **won a national medal last month** ; **trains six days a week for four years** ; **European championships in June**. Opinion : « **I think** I can reach the final ». Sentiment : « **I couldn't believe it** », « **I just cried** » (« honestly » annonce une réponse sincère).
:::
:::

::: exercice 10 | entrainement | 7 min | main
Écouter la phrase dite deux fois avec deux intonations. Dire à chaque fois si la personne est contente ou déçue, et justifier.

::: audio en Two intonations
Version one, said with a rising, bright voice: Oh, great, we're going to the museum again. Version two, said slowly, with a falling voice: Oh, great… we're going to the museum again.
:::

::: corrige
Version 1 : **contente** : voix qui monte, ton vif. Version 2 : **déçue ou ironique** : voix qui tombe, mots traînés, pause après « great ».
Les mots sont identiques ; seule l'intonation change le sens.
:::
:::

::: exercice 11 | entrainement | 7 min | main
Écouter le récit. Répondre en anglais, en phrases complètes : Where did it happen? What went wrong? How did it end? How did the speaker feel at the end?

::: audio en The school trip
Two years ago our class went to Edinburgh by train. Everything went well until the afternoon, when my friend and I got lost in the old town. We didn't have a phone, and it started to rain. Eventually a shopkeeper let us use his phone and our teacher came to get us. I felt embarrassed, but relieved.
:::

::: corrige
It happened **in Edinburgh, in the old town**. **They got lost**, had no phone, and it started to rain. **A shopkeeper let them phone** and **the teacher came to get them**. The speaker felt **embarrassed but relieved**.
« Eventually » signale la fin : au bout d'un moment, la solution arrive.
:::
:::

## Niveau Approfondissement : exercices 12 à 15

::: exercice 12 | approfondissement | 8 min | main
Écouter et déduire : Where is the speaker? What season is it? What is her job? Justifier chaque réponse par deux mots entendus.

::: audio en Guess the situation
Right, everyone, gloves on, please, it's minus twelve tonight. Stay close to the bus and keep your cameras ready. The forecast says the sky will be clear after ten, and with this activity we should see the lights within the hour. Hot chocolate is in the flask at the back.
:::

::: corrige
Where : **in the far north, outdoors, near a bus** (« minus twelve », « close to the bus »). Season : **winter** (« gloves », « minus twelve »). Job : **guide for a northern-lights tour** (« everyone », « cameras ready », « see the lights », « forecast »).
Rien n'est dit directement : chaque déduction s'appuie sur deux indices.
:::
:::

::: exercice 13 | approfondissement | 8 min | main
Écouter l'interview. Classer chaque phrase de la personne interrogée : fait, opinion ou sentiment. Puis expliquer en deux phrases en français pourquoi la question du journaliste est une question d'opinion.

::: audio en A reader's interview
Interviewer: Do you think paper books will disappear?
Reader: I don't think so. I read about forty books a year, half of them on a screen. But when I really love a story, I buy the paper version. There's something about the smell and the weight of a book that makes me happy. In my view, both will survive.
:::

::: corrige
« I read about forty books a year, half on a screen » : **fait**. « When I really love a story, I buy the paper version » : **fait**. « The smell and the weight… makes me happy » : **sentiment**. « I don't think so » et « In my view, both will survive » : **opinions**.
La question du journaliste commence par « Do you think » : elle demande ce que la personne **croit**, pas ce qui est prouvé. Une réponse d'opinion est attendue.
:::
:::

::: exercice 14 | approfondissement | 9 min | main
Écouter le récit inspiré de La Petite Faiseuse de Livres. Tracer la frise, puis répondre : What did Myne want to make? What went wrong the first two times? What finally worked?

::: audio en Making paper
Myne wanted to make paper, because in her town books were only for rich people. First she tried with clay tablets, but they broke when they dried. Then she tried with wood, but it was too heavy to carry. Finally, an old craftsman showed her how to boil plant fibres, press them flat and dry them in the sun. After three weeks, she held her first sheet of paper. It was grey and rough, but she said it was the most beautiful thing she had ever made.
:::

::: corrige
Frise : 1. **wanted paper** (books for rich people). 2. **clay** : broke. 3. **wood** : too heavy. 4. **craftsman** : boil fibres, press, dry in the sun. 5. **first sheet** after three weeks.
She wanted to make **paper**. The **clay tablets broke** and the **wood was too heavy**. **Boiling plant fibres, pressing and drying them** finally worked.
:::
:::

::: exercice 15 | approfondissement | 10 min | main
Écouter le bulletin deux fois. Rédiger en anglais, en six phrases, un résumé des deux nouvelles avec les cinq questions pour chacune, puis une phrase qui donne ton avis, introduite par « In my opinion ».

::: audio en Evening news
Good evening. Thousands of people gathered in Brighton this afternoon to clean the beach after last week's storm. Volunteers collected more than two tonnes of plastic in three hours. The council thanked them and promised more bins for the summer. In other news, a fourteen-year-old girl from Cardiff has won a national science competition with a project on growing vegetables without soil. She will represent Wales at the European final in Berlin in October.
:::

::: corrige
Exemple attendu :
« This afternoon, thousands of volunteers cleaned the beach in Brighton after last week's storm. They collected more than two tonnes of plastic in three hours, and the council promised more bins. In other news, a fourteen-year-old girl from Cardiff won a national science competition. Her project was about growing vegetables without soil. She will go to the European final in Berlin in October. In my opinion, both stories show that young people can change things. »
La réponse est juste si les deux nouvelles sont résumées avec leurs chiffres et si l'avis est séparé des faits.
:::
:::

## Sujet de type devoir

::: exercice 16 | approfondissement | 40 min | main
**Devoir sur 20, en 40 minutes, à la main.** Chaque script peut être écouté trois fois.

**Partie A (7 points).** Écouter le récit. Tracer la frise (cinq événements dans l'ordre), puis répondre : where did it happen, what went wrong, how did it end.

::: audio en Script A : the camping trip
Last August my family went camping by a lake in Scotland. The first day was perfect: we swam, we fished, and my dad cooked on the fire. But during the night, a storm arrived. The wind pulled the tent out of the ground and the rain got everything wet. We ran to the car and slept there. In the morning, the sun came back, and a farmer let us dry our things in his barn. Eventually we stayed three more days, and it was the best holiday ever.
:::

**Partie B (7 points).** Écouter le bulletin. Pour chaque nouvelle, donner who, what, where, when, why, et un chiffre.

::: audio en Script B : the news
A bridge in the centre of York will be closed for repairs from Monday, after engineers found cracks during an inspection last week. The work will last two months. And in science news, astronomers in Chile photographed a new comet on Tuesday night. The comet will be visible without a telescope from the fifteenth of March, just after sunset.
:::

**Partie C (6 points).** Écouter l'interview. Relever deux faits, deux opinions et un sentiment, avec les mots-signaux. Puis dire en une phrase en anglais si la personne est plutôt optimiste ou pessimiste, et pourquoi.

::: audio en Script C : a young baker
Interviewer: You opened your bakery six months ago. How is it going?
Baker: Well, we sell about three hundred loaves a day, which is more than I expected. To be honest, the first month was terrifying. I think the secret is simple: good flour and a lot of patience. In my opinion, people are coming back to real bread. I'm really proud of what we've done.
:::

::: corrige
**Partie A : 7 points**
Frise (1 point par événement dans l'ordre, 5 points) : 1. camping by a lake in Scotland, perfect first day. 2. storm at night, tent pulled out, everything wet. 3. slept in the car. 4. sun came back, farmer's barn to dry things. 5. stayed three more days, best holiday. Réponses (2 points) : in Scotland, by a lake ; a storm destroyed the tent ; they stayed three more days and loved it.

**Partie B : 7 points**
Nouvelle 1 (3,5 points) : who : engineers / the council ; what : a bridge closed for repairs ; where : centre of York ; when : from Monday, for two months ; why : cracks found during an inspection last week ; chiffre : two months. Nouvelle 2 (3,5 points) : who : astronomers ; what : photographed a new comet ; where : in Chile ; when : Tuesday night ; why : visible without a telescope from 15 March, after sunset ; chiffre : the fifteenth of March.

**Partie C : 6 points**
Faits (2 points) : opened six months ago ; sells about three hundred loaves a day. Opinions (2 points) : « I think the secret is… » ; « In my opinion, people are coming back to real bread ». Sentiment (1 point) : « the first month was terrifying » ou « I'm really proud ». Phrase (1 point) : optimistic, because sales are higher than expected and she is proud.
:::
:::
